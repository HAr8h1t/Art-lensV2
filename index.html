Roles

Explorer, creator, and admin workflows

Early dashboard previews show how the same cultural graph supports different users.

Explorer

  • Save traditions, creators, works, sites, events, and workshops.
  • Search structured entities rather than generated answers.
  • Contact or support creators where appropriate.

Creator

  • Build a practitioner profile with region, tradition, story, media, and works.
  • Publish workshops and event participation.
  • Review AI-suggested metadata before anything becomes public truth.

Admin / Moderator

  • Review submissions, sources, verification status, and reported content.
  • Promote records from community submitted to source backed or verified.
  • Keep demo data and cultural claims clearly labelled.

Trust layer

Sources and verification queue

AI may assist with translation and classification, but cultural truth requires attribution and review.
